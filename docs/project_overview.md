# Project overview

Project name: DataLens (formerly FlowLens). Initial focus: CapExLens.

DataLens is a Python platform for financial research and visualization. Every Lens
should be visual, quantifiable, and composable. Structured observations and indicators
support mathematical models; timestamped events preserve nonnumeric economic context.
Each Lens gets the visualization best suited to its data.

V1 starts with one company's capital expenditures: accurate ingestion, standard metrics,
and one interactive historical chart with source-backed details and documented allocations.
Microsoft is the proposed first company. Alphabet and Amazon follow after V1.
MacroLens, GeoLens, EnergyLens, and EarningsLens extend the same contracts. QuantLab
will consume both numerical indicators and economic events for research.

Future networks show relationships changing through time, distinguishing information
known at a historical date from relationships economically effective on that date.
Financial allocations, estimates, and thematic connections remain visually distinct.
Missing allocation evidence is shown as undisclosed, not inferred supplier spending.

Current status: folder structure and placeholders only.
